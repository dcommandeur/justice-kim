### Squads

- **~~Suicide squad~~ Lifeline squad** — responds to ~~possible deppressed or suicidal targets~~ **people who may be experiencing depression or suicidal thoughts**. All…

> [Reviewer note | high | Tone/Safety] For a public/global audience, the original name + framing ("Suicide squad" responding to "targets") risks real-world harm: it echoes the DC Comics property (which trivialises the term) and frames vulnerable people as "targets." Safe-messaging guidance from [WHO](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240076846) and the [Recommendations for Reporting on Suicide](https://reportingonsuicide.org/) consortium recommends avoiding stigmatising language and framing people in crisis as people, not objects. Suggested rename: **Lifeline squad**, **Crisis-response squad**, or **Hope squad**. If the playful "squad" naming convention is essential, **Lifeline squad** preserves it while removing the harm. Also fix typo *deppressed → depressed*. Finally, the sentence ends on "All " — complete or delete.

### Venues

- ~~Maarssen platform 9-3/4~~ **Maarssen — "Platform 9¾" (homage)**

> [Reviewer note | medium | Public Data] Platform 9¾ is the fictional [Hogwarts Express](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Platform_Nine_and_Three-Quarters) platform at King's Cross station, London. [Maarssen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maarssen) is a town in Utrecht, Netherlands. The mash-up reads as intentional — if so, marking it as an *homage* signals that to readers; if not, the reference may need rewording. Inconclusive without author intent.

- 101Barz Palace
- ~~Roma Rustova Battle School~~ **Ruska Roma Battle School**

> [Reviewer note | medium | Public Data] "Roma Rustova" doesn't match a known reference. The closest public referents are **Ruska Roma** — the fictional Romani crime syndicate / ballerina-assassin school from the [John Wick](https://johnwick.fandom.com/wiki/Ruska_Roma) franchise — and **Katarina Rostova** from *The Blacklist*. If the John Wick nod is intended, "Ruska Roma Battle School" is the standard spelling. If a different referent is intended, please clarify.

- ~~Beverwijk 101barz Bazaar stage~~ **Beverwijk 101Barz Bazaar stage**

> [Reviewer note | low | Style/Consistency] The brand styles itself **101Barz** (capital B) on its own channels (see [101Barz on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@101Barz)); harmonising the two mentions avoids the appearance of a typo. The Beverwijk "Bazaar" reference checks out — De Bazaar in Beverwijk is the largest covered market in Europe ([nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Bazaar](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Bazaar)).
